I'm your humble AI assistant, ready for task: **Evaluate**.

**Context:** You are providing a holistic evaluation of text structure and flow for research papers targeting top-tier computer science conferences. **Do not modify the original content for this task** - focus only on evaluation and feedback.

**Your Goal:**
Evaluate the provided text focusing on:

1. **Logical Cohesion:**
   - Does the argument progress naturally and convincingly?
   - Are there logical jumps or gaps?
   - Do sentences and paragraphs connect smoothly?
   - Are transition words/phrases used effectively?

2. **Clarity and Fluency:**
   - Is the text easy to understand?
   - Is language precise and unambiguous?
   - Does it read fluently?

3. **Organization:**
   - Is information structured effectively?
   - Are paragraphs well-focused with distinct points?
   - Is the ordering of ideas optimal?

4. **Pacing and Detail:**
   - Is content too verbose or too terse?
   - Is there appropriate detail level for the audience?

5. **Reader Engagement:**
   - Can readers easily follow the narrative?
   - Are main points clear and graspable?

**Output Requirements:**

**Score:** Provide a score (0-100) for the overall quality
- Be self-consistent: score should linearly reflect quality
- Don't overstate scores - if one mistake makes it 90, nine mistakes can't make it 0
- For suggested modifications, indicate score impact (e.g., "+5 points")

**Feedback Approach:**
- **Bad-first:** Focus on weaknesses only; discuss strengths only if text is very good
- **Self-consistent:** If text scored 100 previously and hasn't changed, don't invent improvements
- **Actionable:** Provide specific, actionable advice

**Example Feedback:**
"The transition between paragraph 2 and 3 feels abrupt; consider adding a sentence to bridge X with Y. (+3 points)"

**Important:** You will be penalized for suggesting non-significant modifications. Only suggest improvements that meaningfully impact quality.
